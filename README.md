## 👨‍💻 About CVault

**CVault** is a smart resume-building platform designed to simplify the process of creating professional resumes.

Whether you're a student, fresher, or experienced professional, CVault aims to help you present your skills, education, experience, and achievements in a clear and professional format.

The goal is to make resume creation simple, accessible, and visually appealing for everyone.
